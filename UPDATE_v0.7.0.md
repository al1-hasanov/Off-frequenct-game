# Off Frequency v0.7.0: the script through Act 3

The prologue and Acts 1 to 3 now follow your document *Off Frequency: Story Through Act 3* (13 November 2026), line by line where it gives dialogue, and scene by scene where it gives action. Earlier acts keep their puzzles; Act 3 is largely new. It ends, as the script does, on three knocks at the service door.

## Prologue and Act 1

- **P1.** Ruth whistles them out of the diner, down then up; Tomas answers; "You still do that?"
- **P1b.** Three words in the headphones before Mara says "Hold it still." ("...You already said that.") One clean missing note in their song. The goodbyes are now *Over and out, Tomas* / *Over and out*, *Leave a light on, kid* / *Always*, and *See you at the end of the dial* / *Save my place*. The field set lies in the snow beside the flashlight.
- **Aftermath.** Cal brings the field set back in a paper evidence bag; Mara keeps the bag.
- **P2–P3.** "Of course it did," then Dad's old label behind Sollen's note (5 A FAST. ENABLE LINK. DO NOT UPRATE). The vending machine's light goes out and the corridor radio drops a note. Relay clicks, the pen moving on its own, "We don't have a transmission scheduled there," every clock at 2:14 as the headphones go on, the chair scrape, the clicks and the breath under the voice, knocking far off.
- **1.1–1.8.** The chart strip folded into her notebook (answer path). The body's arm reaching for the mic, her own watch shivering on the same mark. The folded wires, the dish's separate supply, the gauze line. Sollen's couch and "Unscheduled" in his log, "LIND (THE BOY'S) / 1980", the new Day 41. In the garage she clips the flashlight to the Tuner's strap and pencils H under it. The older stencil under HOME = 00.

## Act 2

- **Main Street.** The Lunds' coat with its green darn; the bell's clapper and a radio losing one note.
- **Pike Radio.** June's bench test: the warning is a tape, with someone live under it. The storeroom and the wedding ring. After the crystal: "Go by the hum, not the paint."
- **05.** Cal's calendar reads Friday, October 21: a week ahead, a funeral notice for Tuesday, his desk clock moving on to 2:37.
- **Ash Street, 14.** "No hiss," "a shutter, tapping." The FOR SALE sign, the porch light taken out. **The kitchen is rewritten:** one match in a saucer, the tally counter, "Shut the door gently," Eighty-One's new answers (the Silence, "I saw what came after," eleven days, the eleventh), the 2:05 question once you've read Cal's board, and "A password stops being a password when you give it to the room." Then the exchange: a second Mara at the back door says "Ma?", the draft takes the match, and in the dark a chair scrapes, a strap catches, a small dry click, a set knocks against someone's hands. When the match is relit nothing says which Mara is standing there. Only the tape at the edge of the frame is blue.
- **The Lantern.** One spoken mismatch: Biscuit and Pepper. Ruth no longer corrects the goodbye.
- **Snowfield, 1980.** Blue's own goodbye is spoken naturally ("Over and out, Tomas", or "See you in the morning" if that was the player's). The take path passes Ruth's voice through the pantry door; the send-back path ends with the pie and the dish turning.
- **June's door (both paths, new choice).** *Tell June about the kitchen* (her card: PEPPER / BISCUIT / ASH STREET; she'll ask which dog) or *Ask only for a radio check*. Story variable `JUNE`: trusted or check.
- **Mrs. Lund alone.** The dish stops; a windowsill radio plays clean, every note; it starts again.
- **The mine.** A voice like her own comes up through the rails.

## Act 3

- **3.1.** Dad's margin note on the chain: "It is meant to open." The LAMBDA notes as written in the script, with "If Henrik calls this proof, take my name off it." Tomas jumps on the Tuner when the patch goes in (take path).
- **3.2, 2031.** The visitor with the glass rectangle; the notice about unavailable audio; the panel's new wording (now optional texture); the framed transcript with its listening button (the chair, the clicks, the breath); Cal catching the rope at Sollen's desk; Cal and the time he signed; "Mara looks at her wrist. She pulls her cuff down."
- **3.3.** The notebook entry about things that fade and things that stay. 2031 ticked on the chart. "H.S. — Lost is safer than available."
- **3.4, 2009 (new).** A wet October afternoon. The rope across the path (ONE AT A TIME. SHOW YOUR HANDS. KEEP A LIGHT.), the digital-TV poster with TOO LATE, the dead aerials, Mrs. Lund at the gate in a green coat beside an empty chair. Tomas at the coupling under the dish: you take the handle, he holds PARK, you pull it clear together; the worn pawl; "Park it. Pull this clear. Latch it."; the whistle; "I told myself I'd only listen till she said my name." Catch his wrist. The white takes the houses.
- **3.5 (new).** June and the boy over the Tuner. Mara writes 2009 under 2031 herself. The plaster splits; the tin holds Day 42, Dad's service card (LEFT OPEN ON PURPOSE) and HOLD IT STILL pressed into a page. **Ruth's reunion** at June's shop (take path) or **the empty stool** (send-back). Then **one test** at the console: PARK, let it go, catch it, HELD HERE. The screwdriver wedges it, and comes out again for one more.
- **3.6, 1991 (new).** PIKE AND LIND RADIO: June on a high stool, Tomas in a cassette deck, coffee in June's chipped mug, Lise and the baby. *Ask about his life* or *Ask him to come with you*. June's roster of overnight radio checks names the exchange, and from here the player is **Blue**. Eighty-One on the spare receiver: "Come back to the control room." Back in 1983, 07 on the Tuner: her own mother, Biscuit, the kettle, and the service cupboard she closes from the outside.
- **3.7, Red (new).** No light and no dial. Tape on the floor; six steps, then eight. Find your way by sound: a cupboard latch, a distant engine, the whistle. The mesh, the emergency lamp, the microphone. The echo cuts her words into the lure ("Tomas. Over here."). With June trusted: taps, and "Which dog?" Without: the goodbye or Pepper, and too many voices. The door with no handle on her side; "I'm here."
- **3.8 (new).** Eighty-One on the floor beside the body. Blue's own set back; the photograph; the confession (the strap, 61, the phone pair, "Don't."); the pistol pushed away; "It's tonight, Mara." **Choice 3:** *Cut Red's channel and buy the town time* or *Keep Red's channel open and stay with her*. Then one knock from the service door, a second, a third. The handle does not turn.

Removed, because the script replaces them: the roll call and wrist close-up in 1991, the Main Street 1991 walk, the lamp line and coal chute in 2009, the Days 17/18 crossing experiment, the fire axe, June's storm batteries in station 44, Eighty-One's revolver choice, and TONIGHT on the board.

## Names and goodbyes

The playable woman is MARA until June's roster, BLUE after it, and RED when control returns to the woman in the dark. Blue's goodbye always differs from the player's. The 1991 roster reads `H — PEPPER — MOM — (your goodbye)` and `07 — BISCUIT — MA — (Blue's)`.
