# Off Frequency v0.6.2: what Dad was listening to

Dark energy is now the science under the story. Anders Lind's Air Force study, code name LAMBDA, was measuring the energy of empty space, twenty years before anyone called it dark energy. The game hints at it in every year Mara visits, and every clue is something that already existed in that year. All of it is optional: nothing new blocks the way forward.

## The clues, year by year

- **1983, Sollen's office.** Dad's old textbook on the shelf, *Gravitation* (Misner, Thorne and Wheeler, 1973), with his LAMBDA working notes folded inside: Einstein's field equation with Λ, the energy of empty space; Einstein and Rosen's bridge between two sheets of space (1935); Everett's "every branch is real" (1957); Gödel's loop into the past (1949); and LIGHT = BALLAST. In Act 3 you can read the notes. In Acts 1 and 2 Mara only notices the book.
- **1983, the control room.** Under Sollen's survey schedule, a circled Λ that four years of erasing never got off, and the faint ghost of the field equation.
- **1983, the Tuner and Tomas's notebook.** Day 1 now says what Dad wrote inside the field set's lid: Λ, LIGHT = BALLAST. Once you've read the notebook, the Tuner's description says it too.
- **Dad's initials.** His diagram is signed Λ.L. 10/79, because he wrote his A as a Greek lambda. Sollen's 1980 log has LAMBDA RUN in the margin beside 02:14.
- **2031, the visitor center.** Einstein's equation in brass letters high on the wall, and its panel: what the dish was listening for, from 1917 to 2025. It covers Λ, the Einstein–Rosen bridge, Gödel, Everett's many worlds (the branches popular books call parallel universes), Zel'dovich's vacuum energy, LAMBDA, Thorne's wormhole time machine (1988), the 1998 discovery of dark energy, the 2011 Nobel Prize, and DESI's 2025 hints that dark energy is weakening. The 2.14 study's placard now calls it a study of the energy of empty space, and Cal tells you to look up.
- **2009, Tomas's hut.** On his door, a 1999 HALVARD COURIER clipping about dark energy, with DAD WAS RIGHT across the top, and the 2003 Big Rip paper ("Here it's small. Here it's the white.").
- **1991, Pike & Lind Radio.** The 1988 paper on wormholes as time machines is pinned over the bench with Tomas's notes ("Dad's thin places push."), and a dog-eared *A Brief History of Time* lies on the boom box.

## Four new theories

Each clue adds a note to Mara's notebook, and four new pairs connect (twelve theories in all):

- Dad's notes and the museum wall: Dad's Λ is dark energy.
- Dad's bridge and branches, and Tomas's "other us" tape: the frequencies are the branches, parallel Halvards side by side.
- Tomas's Big Rip printout and your chart strips: the white is a small Big Rip, and the dish is pulling.
- Tomas's wormhole paper and Dad's relay: Dad found the way through and wired it into a radio.

## The ? button points to them

While a clue in the room you're standing in is still unread, the ? button adds one line after its usual instruction: "Optional: the old chalk under the schedule on the chalkboard", "the black textbook on Sollen's shelf", "the lettering high on the wall" (2031), "the papers on Tomas's hut door" (2009) or "the photocopy pinned over the workbench" (1991). It stops once you've read it, and never appears during Red's dark or after it.

## Red's turnings, easier to follow

- Each sound's name is written in the middle of its rings (LEFT, AHEAD or RIGHT, and what you hear), and the rings are brighter.
- The ? button now knows which turning you're at and tells you where the farm report is.

## Install

`OffFrequency-v0.6.2-debug.apk` installs over v0.6.1 and keeps the save (same package and key, version code 14). Chapters (debug) → Act 3 jumps to any part: 3.1 for Sollen's office and the chalkboard, 3.2 for 2031, 3.4 for 2009, 3.6 for 1991.
