# Off Frequency v0.6.1: true to scale

Act 3's places are now drawn to the people's scale: about 110 pixels to the metre, the same as Mara (1.7 m). Cars are full size, doors are taller than the people who walk through them, and buildings have real storeys that run up out of the picture.

## Outside the observatory (1983, 2031, 2009)

- **Cars at full size,** parked a metre back in the lot, behind the people: Mara's late-'70s sedan (5.4 m), the 2031 electric crossover and its charging post, the abandoned SUV and sedan in 2009 (a little further back, so a little smaller). The 2031 shuttle waits at its stop further back again.
- **The control building** is 4.5 m to its parapet, with two-and-a-quarter-metre glass lobby doors, a ribbon of lab windows at the right height and the air handler and radio mast on the roof. In 2031 the glass entrance box, its sign and its banners are rebuilt to match; in 2009 the ruin's walls break off at every height and the spray paint is full size on the lobby wall.
- **The stone wing** is a real two-storey building (1931, WEATHER POST over its door): tall arched windows on both floors, quoins, a string course, and its eaves above the top of the picture. The lamp line, the string of bulbs and the coal chute's hatch sit where they would on a building that size.
- **The dish** now stands well back across the summit, so distance shrinks it, with a little haze. You walk out to it on the right (tap the dish) to climb to the feed cabin; you come back the same way. In 2009 Tomas walks out to it at 2:14, and the coupling glows in the distant cabin.
- A five-metre sodium lamp over the lot in 1983; the dish's disconnect box on its post where the path starts; Tomas's hut full size, with its door, stovepipe and woodpile.

## Main Street, 1991

- Rebuilt at full scale: three shopfronts fill the picture. The Lantern (two storeys of brick, the big window, the awning, the door with OPEN 6 AM), PIKE & LIND RADIO (its false front, the sign, a shop window of televisions and a boom box, a mesh satellite dish on the wall, June's antenna on the roof), and Halvard Video in the old sheriff's office (COUNTY SHERIFF still cut into the stone). The mountain and the observatory's light show over the rooftops.
- **His sedan and their minivan are parked at the kerb at full size,** nearer to you than the sidewalk, so they're drawn in front of the people.
- The payphone, the newspaper box and two streetlamps are full size too. The torn white no longer covers the clock.

## Inside, in 1991 and 2031

- The back room of Pike & Lind Radio: the computer desk, the workbench, the hot plate's table and the rig's desk are at real desk and bench heights, and June (or Lise) sits in a wing chair of the right size.
- The visitor center: Sollen's desk is at desk height and the card index is a real card cabinet.

## Install

`OffFrequency-v0.6.1-debug.apk` installs over v0.6.0 and keeps the save (same package and key, version code 13). Chapters (debug) → Act 3 jumps to any part: 3.2 for 2031, 3.4 for 2009, 3.6 for 1991.
